FAQ
Common Questions
Everything you need to know before you start.
Is Module 1 really free? Do I need a credit card? +
Yes — Module 1 (3 lessons) of every category is completely free. No credit card needed. Just sign up with your email and start learning immediately.
What is the payment method? Can I use UPI? +
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and all major credit/debit cards via Razorpay. Payment is 100% secure and instant access is granted after payment.
How many lessons are there per course? +
Each category has 12 lessons organized across 4 modules (3 lessons per module). Module 1 is free. Modules 2, 3, and 4 are unlocked after purchase. All lessons have voice narration, written content, and a quiz.
Is the content in Tamil or English? +
The written content and AI prompts are in English (suitable for Indian English learners). The voice narration uses a clear, India-accented English voice. We are working on full Tamil voice support coming soon.
What does "Live AI Prompts — Always Updated" mean? +
The AI Coach tab inside each category uses Claude AI to generate fresh, trend-relevant prompts in real time. When you click a trend tag or press Generate, it creates brand new prompts based on current trends — so the content never goes stale. This is not pre-recorded — it's live AI generation every time.
Is there a subscription? Will I be charged again? +
No subscription. ₹399 gives 6 months access, ₹999 gives 1 year access, and ₹2,499 gives true lifetime access — forever. No hidden charges.
What is the refund policy? +
All sales are final once access is granted — this is a digital product. Exception: If the content doesn't match what was described, or you face a technical issue preventing access, email aikatrukko@gmail.com within 7 days and we will resolve it. Try Module 1 for free before purchasing — no risk.
Do I need any prior knowledge of AI? +
Zero prior knowledge needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use AI Katrukko. Every course starts from basics and builds up. The content is designed for everyday people — students, business owners, job seekers — not tech experts.
Can I buy more than one category? +
Yes! Each category is purchased separately. You can buy Students + Job Seekers if you're a final year student looking for a job, for example. Each category has completely different content built for that audience.
Who is Pooja and why should I trust this course? +
Pooja Gunasekaran is a UX/UI Designer and AI Educator who built AI Katrukko to make practical AI education accessible and affordable for everyday Indians. This is not a generic AI course — every lesson is built around real Indian use cases, real tools, and real results. Try Module 1 free and judge for yourself.
